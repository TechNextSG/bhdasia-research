🛠️ Services (full detail)

🌀 TRE®

Exercises activate the psoas muscles to trigger involuntary neurogenic tremors that discharge deep tension — drawing on psychology, neurology & physiology. Created by Dr. David Berceli.

  • Lie on a mat, simple exercises → natural shaking
  • Training: 3–4 supervised sessions then self-practice
  • “Moderation, not intensity” — overuse → restlessness
  • Widespread in US/Europe; emerging in Asia

🧠 Somatic Coaching

Targeted questioning to develop embodied intelligence — the body’s wisdom. “Understanding is insufficient… learned through experience and practice.”

  • For: burnout, emotional numbness, chronic tension
  • Commitment: 6–12+ sessions, practice between

🤲 Somatic Bodywork

The Strozzi method — a gentle, systematic process to release historical patterns, contractions & tension. “Soma” = the living body in its wholeness.

  • Props: gymnastic balls, soft balls, elastic bands
  • Clients become “living scientists” of their balance
