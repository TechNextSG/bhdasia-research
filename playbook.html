1 · Brand architecture — fix the fragmentation first

The single biggest structural problem: one person, three disconnected domains, none linking to each other. Authority and SEO equity leak everywhere. Before any posting, decide the model.

OptionWhat it meansProsCons
A · Single umbrellaCollapse all three into one brand (likely BHD) with service sub-sectionsMaximum SEO consolidation; simplest to run; one audienceLoses the consumer-friendly "HummingBeing" & Japan-local "Partage" angles
B · Hub-and-spoke RecommendedPersonal brand (Isabelle) is the hub; BHD (corporate), HummingBeing (wellness) & Partage (Japan) are spokes, all cross-linked & sharing one design systemKeeps distinct audiences; founder authority feeds all; clean storytellingMore to maintain; needs shared templates & a content team
C · Status quoThree separate sites, no linksNoneConfusing, dilutes SEO, wastes the founder's authority
Recommendation: Option B. Isabelle's personal profile + a one-page hub (own domain or Linktree-style) becomes the centre; each brand site links up to her and across to siblings. One shared visual system so the three feel like a family, not strangers.

3 · SEO foundation — get found before you get followed

Zero blogs today = near-zero organic discovery. Target intent-rich, low-competition clusters tied to the real services & locations.

Keyword clusters to own

BrandPrimary clustersExample long-tail targets
HummingBeingTRE Singapore · somatic coaching · stress & burnout"TRE practitioner Singapore", "what is somatic bodywork", "trauma release exercises near me"
BHD Asiaexecutive coaching Asia · leadership development · team effectiveness"executive coach Singapore C-suite", "leadership resilience training", "transition coaching"
Partagecoaching Japan · corporate detox · mindfulness Kyoto"executive coach Kyoto English", "corporate mindfulness Japan", "TRE certification Japan"

On-page & technical checklist

  • Unique title + meta description per page
  • One H1, logical H2/H3, descriptive alt text
  • Schema: Organization, Person, Service, FAQ, Review
  • Internal links between blog ↔ service pages
  • Sitemap.xml + robots.txt + Search Console verified
  • Core Web Vitals: <2.5s LCP, image compression
  • Google Business Profile for HummingBeing (local SEO + reviews)
  • Canonical tags on republished Forbes/Brainz articles

4 · Content engine — own the authority you already rent

The Forbes & Brainz bylines are the single most under-used asset. One expert idea should fuel a week of content across every channel — the "create once, publish everywhere" waterfall.

The 4 content pillars

PillarAngleProof source
🌀 Body & stress (TRE/somatic)Educate: how the nervous system holds stress; quick resetsTRE practice, HummingBeing
🧭 Leadership & resilienceAuthority: lessons from coaching C-suitesForbes/Brainz articles, BHD
🌏 Culture & the human at workPOV: burnout, psychological safety, cross-cultural teams27-yr HR career, ACCJ talk
💬 Founder story & proofTrust: the corporate-to-coach journey, client resultsPersonal brand, testimonials

The repurposing waterfall (per source idea)

  • 1 · Pillar article (Forbes/Brainz or new blog, ~1,000 words) →
  • 2 · LinkedIn text post (the core insight, 1,300 chars) →
  • 3 · Carousel (LinkedIn + Instagram, 6–8 slides) →
  • 4 · Short-form video (Reel / TikTok / YouTube Short, 30–60s) →
  • 5 · Newsletter section (LinkedIn newsletter + email) →
  • 6 · Quote graphic for fill-in days.

Lead magnets to build

  • "Leadership Resilience Playbook" (BHD, B2B)
  • "5-Minute Nervous-System Reset" (HummingBeing, B2C)
  • "Beating Burnout: a manager's checklist" (Partage / corporate)

5 · Platform playbooks — cadence, format & sample posts

Each channel has a job. Don't post the same thing everywhere — adapt to the platform's native format.

in · LinkedIn — the engine (personal + company)

Job: authority + inbound B2B leads. Cadence: personal 4×/week; BHD company page 3×/week + employee reshares. Baseline: ~14–33 reactions/post, BHD page 22 followers.

Format mix (personal, weekly)

  • Mon — insight/POV text post (leadership/resilience)
  • Wed — carousel or client-proof story
  • Fri — short-form video (TRE / somatic explainer)
  • Sun — founder-story or "behind the work" reflection
  • Launch a LinkedIn newsletter (monthly, native reach to all followers)
// Sample LinkedIn hook (Pillar: Leadership & resilience) "Most executives try to *think* their way out of burnout. After coaching 100+ C-suite leaders across 11 countries, here's what actually works — and it starts in the body, not the calendar. 🧵" → 4–6 short lines, 1 takeaway, soft CTA to the blog/booking.

📸 Instagram

Job: HummingBeing wellness brand + humanise Isabelle. Cadence: 3–4×/week (2 Reels, 1 carousel, 1 story-set/day).

  • Reels: TRE tremor demos, "do this when stressed", myth-busting
  • Carousels: "3 signs of burnout", somatic tips
  • Stories: behind-the-session, testimonials, polls
  • Hashtags: mix niche (#TRE #somatichealing) + local (#singaporewellness)
  • Bio link → HummingBeing booking

▶ YouTube + 🎵 TikTok (short-form)

Job: searchable evergreen + reach. Cadence: 2 Shorts/week + 1 long video/month.

  • Shorts: repurpose Reels (cross-post)
  • Long: "What is TRE? Full explainer", guided sessions
  • YouTube doubles as SEO (2nd-biggest search engine)
  • TikTok for top-of-funnel discovery (wellness trends)

✉ Email / Newsletter

Job: owned audience → bookings. Cadence: 1 broadcast/2 weeks + welcome sequence.

  • Welcome series (3 emails) on each lead magnet
  • Bi-weekly: one pillar insight + one CTA
  • Back the Partage newsletter promise with real content
  • Segment B2B (BHD) vs B2C (HummingBeing)

🗣 Directories & PR

Job: credibility + referral SEO.

  • Upgrade Noomii off free plan → unhide bio & reviews
  • Keep publishing on Forbes Coach Council & Brainz
  • Pitch ACCJ & Japan/SG chambers for talks/webinars
  • Activate Peatix for every workshop (it's dormant)

6 · The 90-day launch calendar

Foundation first (you can't pour traffic into a leaky site), then content, then social amplification — overlapping, not strictly sequential.

Wk
1–4
Month 1 · Foundation

Architecture decision + site rebuild + tracking

Lock Option B. Rebuild HummingBeing & BHD first (highest intent), wire analytics + pixels + Search Console + Google Business Profile. Set up brand handles & a unified visual kit. Draft 3 lead magnets.

Wk
3–8
Month 2 · Content engine

Blogs live + republish authority + email automation

Stand up all blogs; republish 6–8 Forbes/Brainz pieces (canonical-tagged); publish 4 new pillar articles. Launch welcome email sequences. Begin the repurposing waterfall → first 2 weeks of social queued.

Wk
5–12
Month 3 · Social amplification

Full posting cadence + short-form video + LinkedIn newsletter

All channels at target cadence. Ship 24+ short-form videos. Launch the LinkedIn newsletter. Start employee/founder advocacy on the BHD page. Begin light retargeting once there's traffic to retarget.

90+
Ongoing · Optimise

Measure → double down

Monthly dashboard review; kill what doesn't convert, scale what does; layer paid social against proven organic winners.

SEO
